# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-28

First public release.

- Camera2 viewfinder with RAW (DNG) + JPEG capture, independent auto/manual ISO and shutter,
  autofocus with tap-to-focus and manual focus, white balance presets and Kelvin, exposure
  compensation, AE lock, flash/torch, zoom, timer, grid, level, live histogram
- Metadata (GPS, device, lens, exposure, author, copyright) stored in SQLite, EXIF and XMP
- Non-destructive editor: light, colour, detail, effects and geometry tools, undo/redo,
  version history, compare with original, JPEG export with EXIF
- Gallery with filters, photo details, rating, notes, exported-files screen
- Share and save-to-device for exports and DNG files, optional automatic copy to the phone gallery
- Settings: theme, pure black, accent colour / Material You, text size, author, capture options,
  storage overview, camera diagnostics
- Material 3 UI with Roboto and Lucide icons, English and Bulgarian
